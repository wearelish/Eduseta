Haryana Admission Guide

Haryana Counselling Process

Step 1: Registration

Visit the official Haryana counselling portal and register with your JEE/12th marks.

Step 2: Document Verification

Upload required documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable).

Step 3: Choice Filling

Select your preferred colleges and courses in order of priority.

Step 4: Seat Allotment

Based on your rank and choices, seats will be allotted. Check the result on the portal.

Step 5: Reporting to College

Visit the allotted college with original documents and pay the admission fee within the deadline.

Important Dates

Keep checking the official portal for counselling dates, which typically start in June-July.
