About Us

Project Objective

Haryana College Finder is a comprehensive platform designed to help students find the best colleges in Haryana based on their preferences, budget, and course requirements.

Our Mission

We aim to simplify the college selection process by providing detailed information about colleges, courses, fees, placements, and admission criteria all in one place.

Features

  • Search and filter colleges by city, type, and budget
  • Browse courses: B.Tech, MBA, Polytechnic, BA/BSc/BCom
  • Compare multiple colleges side-by-side
  • Detailed college information including fees, placements, and admission criteria
  • Step-by-step admission guide for Haryana counselling
